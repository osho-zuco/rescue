# Druto - Product Vision

> Digital loyalty stamp cards that actually work.

## The Problem

Physical stamp cards are broken:
- **Lost cards** - Customers forget them at home or lose them entirely
- **Fraud** - Easy to fake stamps or duplicate cards
- **No insights** - Merchants have zero data on customer behavior
- **Manual tracking** - Staff forget to stamp, customers forget to ask
- **No re-engagement** - Can't remind customers to come back

Digital alternatives exist but fail small merchants:
- **Complex setup** - POS integration, hardware requirements
- **Expensive** - Monthly fees that don't make sense for small cafes
- **Overkill** - Full CRM systems when merchants just want stamp cards

## The Solution: Druto

A simple, elegant digital stamp card system:

1. **Merchant displays QR code** at checkout
2. **Customer scans QR** with Druto app
3. **Customer pays via UPI** (any app - GPay, PhonePe, etc.)
4. **Stamp auto-collected** - No manual work
5. **Reward unlocked** when target reached

### Why "Druto"?

Sanskrit for "swift/fast" - reflects the instant, frictionless experience.

## Business Model

### Merchant Subscription (SaaS)

| Plan | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Starter** | Free | 1 reward, 50 stamps/month, basic analytics |
| **Growth** | ₹499/month | Unlimited rewards, unlimited stamps, full analytics |
| **Pro** | ₹999/month | Multi-location, team access, API access |

### Why Subscription (Not Commission)

1. **Predictable revenue** - MRR is easier to forecast than transaction %
2. **Aligned incentives** - We want merchants to get MORE stamps, not fewer
3. **Simpler pricing** - No complex calculations or payment splitting
4. **No payment handling** - Customer pays merchant directly via UPI

### Unit Economics

- CAC target: ₹500 (content marketing + referrals)
- LTV target: ₹6,000 (12 months × ₹500 ARPU)
- LTV:CAC ratio: 12:1

## Key Decisions

### 1. UPI-Based (Not Payment Processing)

**Decision:** Customer pays merchant directly via UPI. We don't touch the money.

**Why:**
- No payment gateway fees (2-3% saved)
- No PCI compliance burden
- Simpler merchant onboarding
- Works with existing payment setup

**Trade-off:** We can't verify if payment actually happened. We trust the scan + location as proxy for visit.

### 2. Geofenced Stamp Collection

**Decision:** Stamps can only be collected within 50m of merchant location.

**Why:**
- Prevents fraud (scanning QR from photo at home)
- Ensures actual visit
- 50m buffer for GPS inaccuracy

**Implementation:**
```
if (distance(userLocation, merchantLocation) > 50m) {
  reject("Please visit the restaurant to collect stamp")
}
```

### 3. One Stamp Per Day Per Merchant

**Decision:** A customer can only collect 1 stamp per merchant per day.

**Why:**
- Prevents gaming (multiple scans in one visit)
- Encourages repeat visits over time
- Aligns with "loyalty" concept

**Edge case:** Customer visits for lunch AND dinner → Only 1 stamp. This is intentional - we're rewarding visit frequency, not spend.

### 4. Mid-Streak Protection

**Decision:** When a customer starts collecting stamps for a reward, the terms are "locked in" even if merchant changes the reward later.

**Example:**
- Day 1: Customer starts card for "5 stamps = Free Coffee"
- Day 3: Merchant changes to "8 stamps = Free Tea"
- Customer's existing card: Still "5 stamps = Free Coffee"
- New customers: Start with new terms

**Why:**
- Fair to customers who started under old terms
- Prevents bait-and-switch
- Builds trust

**Implementation:** `StampCard` has `lockedStampsRequired`, `lockedRewardTitle`, `lockedExpiryDays` fields.

### 5. Stamp Expiry (Optional)

**Decision:** Merchants can optionally set stamp expiry (e.g., "stamps expire after 30 days").

**Why:**
- Creates urgency for customers
- Prevents infinite accumulation
- Optional - some merchants prefer no expiry

**Default:** No expiry (null)

### 6. No Firebase Auth

**Decision:** Build our own OTP auth with Twilio/MSG91 provider abstraction.

**Why:**
- Full control over auth flow
- No Firebase dependency/lock-in
- Can switch OTP providers easily (cost optimization)
- Better error handling and UX

### 7. Payment Gateway Abstraction

**Decision:** Abstract payment gateway behind interface, implement Razorpay and Cashfree.

**Why:**
- Can negotiate better rates by switching
- Redundancy if one provider has issues
- Different providers for different use cases
